Networks and Analysis

UNIT–I: Network Fundamentals and Circuit Analysis Techniques
Basic circuit elements, voltage and current sources, Ohm’s law, Kirchhoff’s Current Law (KCL), Kirchhoff’s Voltage Law (KVL), series and parallel circuits, source transformation, mesh analysis, nodal analysis, network theorems and circuit simplification techniques.

UNIT–II: Network Theorems and Applications
Superposition theorem, Thevenin’s theorem, Norton’s theorem, maximum power transfer theorem, reciprocity theorem, compensation theorem, Millman’s theorem, substitution theorem, applications of network theorems in electrical circuits.

UNIT–III: Transient Analysis
First-order circuits, RL and RC circuits, transient response, natural response, forced response, time constants, switching circuits, second-order RLC circuits, underdamped, overdamped and critically damped responses, transient analysis using differential equations.

UNIT–IV: Sinusoidal Steady-State Analysis
Sinusoidal excitation, phasor representation, impedance and admittance, AC circuit analysis, series and parallel resonance, resonance characteristics, bandwidth, quality factor, power in AC circuits, power factor improvement and three-phase circuit basics.

UNIT–V: Network Functions and Two-Port Networks
Network functions, poles and zeros, driving-point and transfer functions, frequency response, concept of stability, two-port network parameters, Z-parameters, Y-parameters, h-parameters, ABCD parameters, interconnection of two-port networks and practical applications.
